The Archives

Library & Reading Room

Where the museum's written memory lives

Housed in the museum's east wing, our library holds manuscripts, photographs, maps, and oral history recordings gathered over generations. Browse the collection below, or visit in person to consult the archive with our staff.

Reading Room Hours

  • Closed Mondays and public holidays

Access & Requests

Most of the general collection is open for browsing. Rare manuscripts and fragile photographs require a request submitted at least 48 hours in advance through the front desk or by emailing the archive team.
